What to select? Real-world examples

Frequently viewed videos or active data

→ S3 Standard

Access patterns vary unpredictably

→ Intelligent-Tiering

Rarely opened but must open immediately

→ Standard-IA or Glacier Instant, depending on archival needs and retention

Reproducible backup in one AZ

→ One Zone-IA

Archived records; hours acceptable

→ Glacier Flexible

7-year compliance archive

→ Glacier Deep Archive

Extremely latency-sensitive workloads

→ S3 Express One Zone

Worked practice question: 24-hour analytics results

Scenario: Netflix-style analytics pipeline stores intermediate query output for only 24 hours. Other jobs read the files repeatedly.

Answer: S3 Standard. No minimum storage duration charge and no per-GB retrieval fee. For frequently read data kept briefly, the total bill can be lower than using IA or Glacier.

Why the distractors lose

  • Standard-IA: 30-day minimum + retrieval charges.
  • One Zone-IA: Same 30-day minimum + retrieval charges, with single-AZ resilience.
  • Glacier Instant: 90-day minimum + retrieval charges. “Instant” means retrieval is fast, not that the data should be frequently read.
After 24 hours, the files are not automatically deleted. Use application deletion or an S3 Lifecycle expiration rule; expiration is not guaranteed at precisely the 24-hour mark.

Common exam traps

Infrequent access vs short retention

Infrequent access means rarely read, not deleted quickly. Minimum storage-duration charges can dominate a one-day workload.

Glacier Instant vs Glacier Flexible

Glacier Instant retrieves in milliseconds. Glacier Flexible requires restore, typically minutes to hours, and has retrieval tiers.

One Zone vs multi-AZ

One Zone-IA and Express One Zone store in one Availability Zone. Others listed here offer resilience to an AZ loss.

Intelligent-Tiering is not free tiering

Eligible objects have a per-object monitoring/automation fee. Small objects below 128 KB aren't automatically tiered. Optional archive tiers require restores.

Storage cost vs total cost

Compare storage, GET/PUT request cost, retrieval and restore, transfers, transition charges, minimum billable object sizes and retention floors.

Lifecycle vs automatic deletion

Storage classes do not delete objects when their minimum billing period ends. Configure lifecycle expiration separately.
