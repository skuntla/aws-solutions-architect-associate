AWS Backup and DR

Backup planning and disaster recovery strategies.

When to use

Backup/restore is generally least expensive and slowest to recover. Pilot light, warm standby and active-active trade cost for recovery speed.

Simple example

Warm standby runs a smaller functional environment ready to scale up.

Common exam trap

RPO means acceptable data loss; RTO means acceptable recovery time.
